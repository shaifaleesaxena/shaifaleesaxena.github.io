This paper studies latent out-of-distribution detection for deciding when a hybrid controller should use a fast reinforcement-learning policy and when it should switch to robust bounded extremum seeking. A variational autoencoder is trained on in-distribution beam-profile observations, and Mahalanobis distance in the learned latent space provides an interpretable test-time switching signal.

**Status:** Accepted at the ICML RLxF Workshop 2026.
