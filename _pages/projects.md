## Current research projects

### Robust Deep RL for Time-Varying Control (Particle Accelerator Tuning)
This project studies hybrid learning-and-control methods that combine deterministic actor-critic reinforcement learning with bounded extremum seeking for robustness under drift and nonstationarity. I am also exploring safety-aware supervision that detects out-of-distribution operation and determines when to stop trusting the learned policy.

[Read the ACC 2026 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02490)

### Robotic Manipulation under Distribution Shift
I develop hybrid RL and extremum-seeking controllers for robotic manipulation under changes in goals, contact conditions, observations, and dynamics. The work includes OOD-aware switching, online adaptation, multi-task policies, and evaluation across push, grasp, and pick-and-place tasks.

[Read the ICML RLxF Workshop 2026 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01142)

### Vision Representation Learning for RL
This work builds autoencoder and latent-dynamics pipelines that compress high-dimensional visual observations into compact states for downstream prediction, reinforcement learning, and control. The goal is to improve scalability while preserving task-relevant information.

### Space Robotics: Solar Panel Deployment Failure Detection and Repair
I work on vision-based failure detection for satellite solar-panel deployment and on learning-based corrective-control strategies for recovery from jammed or stuck mechanisms.

## Prior engineering and research work

### Flight-Control Simulation and EFCS Development
At Airbus, I worked on electronic flight-control-system packages for training simulators, malfunction logic, validation workflows, and technical support for control and stability behavior.

### UAV Modeling, Estimation, and Control
At Tata Advanced Systems, I worked on nonlinear six-degree-of-freedom aircraft modeling, autopilot development, Total Energy Control System design, extended Kalman filtering, system identification, and flight-performance analysis.

### Swarm Robotics and Autonomous Systems
My earlier engineering work included swarm algorithms for quadcopters in disaster-relief scenarios, obstacle avoidance, and sensing pipelines using LiDAR and onboard systems.

### Structural Health Monitoring
My undergraduate research studied guided-wave-based online damage detection in aerospace structures using piezoelectric actuators and sensors, finite-element modeling, and experimental validation.
